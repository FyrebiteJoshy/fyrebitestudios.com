The Secrets of Hope

An emotional journey through memory, life, and loss.

Get it on Steam

Our first game.

Follow a man coming to terms with a terminal cancer diagnosis as he revisits the memories, people, and moments that shaped his life. Explore surreal environments in this intimate first-person narrative experience about what we leave behind — and where we find hope.

A note from the studio: The Secrets of Hope was our first release and will always mean a lot to us. We don't actively update it anymore, but it remains available on Steam for anyone who wants to experience it.

What kind of game is it

A 2–3 hour story-driven experience for fans of That Dragon Cancer, Gone Home, Dear Esther, and Firewatch.

Explore

Wander through atmospheric environments shaped by memory, emotion, and the moments that defined a life.

Discover

Step away from the main path to uncover optional details, hidden areas, and pieces of the story waiting to be found.

Reflect

Experience an intimate narrative about life, loss, terminal illness, and finding hope when time is running out.
